Support

How to get help with Greenroom.

Pick the way that suits you. A real person reads every message.

Ways to reach us

  • Email: sibhi.gv@gmail.com.
  • In the app: press the Report button in Greenroom and say what is broken, confusing or could be better.
  • GitHub: open an issue on GitHub issues.
  • Read first: the guide covers most questions, and Zoom setup covers using your own Zoom apps. To add, use or remove Greenroom on Zoom, see the guide section.

Support hours

Support is open Monday to Friday, India Standard Time (UTC+5:30). We reply within 2 business days.

What to include

  • The Greenroom version number.
  • Your macOS version.
  • What you pressed, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • Never send passwords, tokens, API keys or Zoom meeting passcodes. We will never ask for them.
