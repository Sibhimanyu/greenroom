The plain terms for using Greenroom.

Who this covers

These terms cover the Greenroom app for macOS and the small signing service it uses when you sign in with Zoom. Greenroom is published by Sibhimanyu G, an individual developer ("we", "us"). Contact: sibhi.gv@gmail.com.

What Greenroom is

Greenroom is a free macOS app for people who host Zoom meetings, such as teachers and presenters. It starts and controls your Zoom meeting through Zoom's Meeting SDK, arranges your camera and screen into one picture, records the session on your Mac, and shows feedback about it. It is not a Zoom product and Zoom does not endorse it.

The source code is public on GitHub, so you can read how it works.

Using it

  • You may use Greenroom for free, for your own meetings, on Macs you control.
  • You need a Zoom account to host a meeting. Zoom's own terms and privacy statement apply to your meetings, your account and everything that goes through Zoom. Greenroom does not change them.
  • You are responsible for your meetings: who joins, what is said, what is shown, and what you record.

Recording and consent

Greenroom can record a session, including your screen, your voice and other people's faces and voices. Before you record, make sure you have the consent the law, your school or your employer requires. Tell the people in the meeting. A recording stays on your Mac, and what you do with it after that is up to you.

What you must not do

  • Do not use it to harass, impersonate or deceive anyone.
  • Do not try to break, overload or get around the signing service, or use it for anything but signing in to Greenroom.
  • Do not break Zoom's terms, or use Greenroom in a way that does.
  • Do not present Greenroom, or a changed copy of it, as an official Zoom product.

If you do, we may stop the signing service from working for you.

Feedback such as pace and facing the room is measured by simple rules on your Mac and is a guide, not a judgement. Keep your own backups of recordings you care about.

Your data

What Greenroom handles, where it goes, and how to delete it is in the privacy policy. In short: recordings stay on your Mac unless you turn on an upload, and we do not sell data.

Stopping

You can stop at any time. In Greenroom, choose Settings, Zoom, Sign out. To remove the app's access in Zoom, sign in to the Zoom Marketplace, click My Library in the bottom-left corner, find Greenroom, click the More icon under Actions, then click Remove and confirm (Zoom's own steps). The steps are the same in the Zoom Workplace app under its Marketplace tab. Then delete the app and its folders as described in the privacy policy. We may end or change the app or the signing service. We will note it on the site and in the release notes.
