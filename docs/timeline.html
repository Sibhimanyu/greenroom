Changelog · 18 releases · Aug 6 – Sep 4, 2026

From a script to a stage, in four weeks.

Greenroom started as the one-click version of a teacher's 7:50 ritual. Every version since was cut from a real morning class: something broke, something was missing, and the fix shipped before the next one. This is the whole record, including the build that never launched.

01 · The shape of it

Two bursts, a steady climb, and a second wind
