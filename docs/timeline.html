From a script to a stage, in fifteen weeks.

Greenroom started as the one-click version of a teacher's 7:50 ritual. Every version since was cut from a real morning class: something broke, something was missing, and the fix shipped before the next one. This is the whole record, including the build that never launched.

01 · The shape of it

Two bursts, a steady climb, a launch weekend, and a rebuild that doubled the app again

Lines of Swift in the app at each release, on the day it shipped. The size of the code is a blunt measure, but it shows where the work went: a first day that produced most of the app, ten days of hardening in the 0.3 series, one Sunday (0.4.0) that doubled it, and two September mornings that gave it a browser and a way onto YouTube. Then the two largest releases of all: a console for the class (0.8.0), and Screenroom (0.9.0), which added more code than the whole app had in its first week. After the 1.0 launch the line goes flat for a month, then 1.3.0 deletes OBS and builds the class picture inside Greenroom, and 1.4.1 to 1.5.0 take the app from 61.5k to 82.6k lines: Greenroom's own meeting window, nine View windows, and the editor and session pages.
