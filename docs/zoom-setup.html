Setup · your own Zoom apps

Connect Greenroom to your own Zoom apps

This is how everyone connects Greenroom to Zoom for now. Sign in with Zoom is coming soon, once Zoom approves Greenroom's Zoom app.

Before you start

Who needs this

  • Everyone, for now. Sign in with Zoom shows as Coming soon until Zoom approves Greenroom's Zoom app: today it can only start meetings on the account that owns that app.
  • The setup guide's Connect Zoom step has one big button, Set up for me. Greenroom does the clicking in Zoom for you. Guide me instead is the same steps, one press at a time. Settings → Zoom → Set up… opens it.
  • You need a Zoom account that may build apps and add admin scopes. Ask your Zoom admin if those options are missing.
  • Both apps must belong to the same Zoom account. About ten minutes.

Official Zoom docs: Meeting SDK credentials · Server-to-Server OAuth. Zoom may rename its screens.

The easy way

Set up for me

In Greenroom's setup guide, press Set up for me. A small form asks for your name, your email and a company name (it fills in your Mac's name; you type the email). These are what Zoom asks for on the hosting app's Information page. If you already have a Meeting app or a Hosting app in Zoom, the form also asks, for each one, Use my existing app or Build a new one. Then press Start. Zoom App Marketplace opens in a large window of its own, and Greenroom works through the steps below. Sign in to Zoom there yourself, as you would anywhere; Greenroom carries on once you are in.

  • Greenroom's bar at the top of the window counts the steps, such as Step 6 of 8 · Add the permissions, and has a Stop button the whole time. Stop ends the run after the action in progress and hands over to the guided steps.
  • Greenroom presses only the controls a step needs, and checks that each press worked before it goes on. It never presses Regenerate, Delete, Remove, Deactivate, Disable, Submit, Publish, Agree, Accept, Terms or Sign out, and it never signs in or accepts terms for you.
  • If something is yours to do (signing in, a page Greenroom doesn't recognise, a step that keeps failing, a press that didn't work), Greenroom stops and the guided step says where it stopped and why. After a step that still fails the check three times, it hands over rather than going round again.
  • When it is done, Check Zoom setup has passed for both apps and the bar offers Back to Greenroom. The check also runs by itself after each app's keys are copied. If it finds something wrong, the step it names is opened again.

If Zoom won't let your account edit the app. On the hosting app's Information page, Zoom may show every field greyed out. Your account is then not allowed to edit Server-to-Server apps. Greenroom stops on a step called Ask your Zoom admin for permission. Ask your Zoom admin to allow Server-to-Server OAuth app: Edit (User Management → Roles → Role Settings → Advanced features), then press Check again.

The other way

Guide me instead

Press Guide me instead to open the same Zoom window and do the same steps one at a time. Greenroom's bar shows the step and one button for it. Each step opens its own page when you are somewhere else (Open Embed, Open Scopes and so on). Do this step does that one step for you, using the same safe presses as Set up for me. Where Greenroom may not press (signing in, Google's Try another way), a ring on Zoom's page marks the control and a one-line note says what to do. Hide tips (or the Tips button) turns the ring off.

The eight steps, in this order. Each app is built completely first and its keys are copied last, because Zoom can change an app's keys when its settings change.

  1. Create the meeting app. It lets Greenroom join and run meetings. If you already have a General app, Greenroom offers Use this app on Zoom's Created apps page.

  2. Turn on Meeting SDK. Without it Zoom won't let the app join meetings. Greenroom turns the switch on if it is off.

  3. Copy its keys. The Client ID and Client Secret, for Greenroom to sign in to meetings. Greenroom reads the ID from the page and presses Zoom's own Copy for the secret; the value goes straight into Greenroom, not through your clipboard.

  4. Create the hosting app. A Server-to-Server OAuth app named Greenroom Hosting. It lets Greenroom create and start your meetings as host. An existing one can be used the same way.

  5. Fill in Information. Zoom asks for a short description, a company name and a contact name and email before it turns the app on. Greenroom types your confirmed values into the empty required fields only, then presses Continue.

  6. Add the permissions. The eight scopes (listed below) let Greenroom create, list, start and end meetings. Greenroom searches for each missing one, ticks it and presses Done. You can also press Copy scopes and add them yourself.

  7. Turn it on. Zoom only answers an app that is switched on. Greenroom presses Activate.

  8. Copy its keys. The Account ID, Client ID and Client Secret, for Greenroom to talk to Zoom for you.

Then Check your setup makes sure both apps really work, and Back to Greenroom finishes.

  • Zoom shows a Server-to-Server app's App Credentials tab first. Greenroom takes you past it to Information, and comes back to it at the end.
  • If the app you open or pick in Zoom is the other one of its kind, Greenroom follows the app you chose.
  • The sign-in is remembered in Greenroom (separate from Safari) until Zoom ends it; Sign out of Zoom (in the bar's … menu, shown only while you are signed in) clears it. Open in Safari is in the same menu.
  • Passkeys aren't available inside Greenroom's Zoom window. If Google offers one, press Try another way and use your password, or sign in in Safari; the sign-in is remembered afterwards.
  • To switch apps, press Use a different app on a finished app's line (it clears that app's keys; Undo is right there). To begin again, press Start over… under the list, or in Settings → Zoom: it forgets both apps' keys on this Mac and signs out of Zoom here. That only removes them from Greenroom. To revoke an app fully, delete or deactivate it in Zoom.
  • Prefer to paste the keys yourself? Open Enter the keys yourself in the setup guide, or follow the by-hand steps below.
By hand · app 1 of 2

Meeting SDK app

Powers Greenroom's built-in meeting client and the chat window.

  1. Open Zoom App Marketplace → Build App

    Sign in. Choose Build App, then General App.

  2. Open your app (Build App page)

    Zoom's deep links need your app's ID, which exists only after you create it. Open your new app from this page.

    Go to Features → Embed. Turn on Meeting SDK.

  3. Zoom docs: Meeting SDK credentials

    Open the app's credentials. Copy the Client ID and Client Secret.

  4. In Greenroom, open the setup guide's Connect Zoom → 1 · Meeting app (Settings → Zoom → Set up…). Paste both values.

    The setup guide's Zoom step (the ? on the main window) has the same fields under Meeting and chat.

Same account only. This app works only in meetings hosted under the Zoom account that owns it. A meeting from another account fails to join. Meetings Greenroom starts for you count as same-account.

By hand · app 2 of 2

Server-to-Server OAuth app

Powers New meeting, the scheduled list and hosting your own meetings.

  1. Open Zoom App Marketplace → Build App

    Choose Server-to-Server OAuth. Name it, then create it.

    Zoom docs: Server-to-Server OAuth

  2. Open your app (Build App page)

    Deep links need your app's ID, so open the new app from this page.

    On App Credentials, copy the Account ID, Client ID and Client Secret.

  3. In Greenroom, open the setup guide's Connect Zoom → 2 · Hosting app. Paste all three.

    The setup guide has them under Create and host meetings.

  4. Open your app's Scopes tab (via the Build App page)

    Add all 8 scopes below, then save. See the next section.

  5. Open your app's Activation tab (via the Build App page)

    Finish the Information page. Then open Activation and activate the app.

Scopes

The 8 scopes to add

Zoom's scope picker searches one name at a time. Copy a scope, paste it into the search, pick the match.

0 of 8 added
  • meeting:write:meeting:adminCreate meetings
  • meeting:read:list_meetings:adminList scheduled meetings
  • meeting:read:meeting:adminRead meeting details
  • meeting:update:meeting:adminEdit scheduled meetings
  • meeting:delete:meeting:adminDelete scheduled meetings
  • user:read:token:adminGet your hosting token
  • meeting:update:status:adminEnd meetings for everyone
  • user:read:user:adminRead your personal room

Ticks are for your own tracking. They live on this page only and are gone when you leave it.

Zoom's older classic apps grant older names instead. Greenroom accepts those too.

Connect

Check Zoom setup

  1. Paste the credentials in the setup guide's Connect Zoom step (the ? on the main window, or Settings → Zoom → Edit setup…).

  2. In the setup guide, press Check Zoom setup. End any running session first.

  3. Read the results. Press Check again after each fix.

Twelve checks run. The permission checks only read what Zoom granted. No meeting is created, joined or ended.

If something fails

Troubleshooting

  • A scope is missing. Add it on the app's Scopes page, save, then press Check again. Greenroom's error names the scope.
  • App not activated. Finish Information, open Activation, activate. The hosting check passes only then.
  • Wrong account. Both apps must sit under the Zoom account that hosts your meetings. Otherwise joins fail.
  • Secret changed or expired. If you regenerated a secret or deactivated the app, hosting fails. Paste the new value.
  • "Zoom details changed during the check." You edited a field mid-check. Press Check again.
  • Check button is disabled. A session is running. End it first.
On your Mac

What stays on your Mac

  • Your Client IDs, secrets and Account ID are saved in Greenroom's local preferences. Not the macOS Keychain.
  • They are not encrypted. Someone using your Mac account could read them.
  • They go only to Zoom, to sign in and call its API.
  • An exported settings file (Settings → Transfer → Export) carries these secrets in plain text. Greenroom asks before it writes one. Share it carefully.
  • Sign in with Zoom (coming soon) is different. Its refresh token sits in the Keychain, and no secret is pasted. See the privacy policy.
